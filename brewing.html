Brewing

It's the water, not the tea

Almost every bad cup of matcha in India is a temperature problem. Tencha scorches above 85°C, releasing catechins that taste harsh and grassy. Below 70°C it never gives up its sweetness. The window is narrow and nobody puts it on the packet.

No thermometer? Boil the kettle, then wait two minutes with the lid off. That lands you around 78°C almost every time.

The method, per powder

१

Matchaमाचा

Sift 2g through a fine strainer into a wide bowl. Skip this and you get lumps. Heat water to 78°C — boil it, then wait two minutes.

२

Hojichaहोजिचा

Sift 3g into a bowl or mug. Heat water to about 88°C — hojicha takes hotter water than matcha.

३

Ubeऊबे

Whisk 10g with 40ml warm water into a smooth paste. Add 200ml milk, hot or cold. Oat works better than almond here.

४

Sachetsपुड़िया

Tear one sachet into a mug — no scale needed, it is already 2g. Add 60ml water at 78°C.
