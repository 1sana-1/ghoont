The story · कहानी

Nobody hates matcha. They hate bad matcha.

एक घूंट, ठीक से बना हुआ।

Three things ruin nearly every cup made at home: boiling water, a culinary grade sold as ceremonial, and powder ground eight months ago by someone who won't say when. None of them are the tea's fault, and all three are fixable.

That's the whole brief. Buy properly, date it honestly, and keep explaining the temperature until people stop making the same bad cup twice.

The name is the smallest unit of the thing — one mouthful. Not a ceremony, not a ritual you need permission to perform. Just a good ghoont, made in about forty seconds.

We're small. The first run is a hundred tins. We'll get things wrong and post about it when we do, because a brand that only shows you the good days isn't telling you much.
