How to make it

Four steps, forty seconds

Sift 2g through a fine strainer into a wide bowl. Skip this and you get lumps.
Heat water to 78°C — boil it, then wait two minutes.
Add 60ml water. Whisk briskly in a W motion for 20–30 seconds until foam forms.
Drink it within a couple of minutes, before the foam falls.
